Timing

      
New episodes are downloaded automatically, from the day the series gets a service.

      

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

      

      

      

      When the service publishes new episodes, from the day Sonarr says they air: 03:00 for Apple TV+, or days before when a platform has them ahead of the channel. Unshackle tries then, and every 30 s for 10 min. No time: the regular syncs (every 2 h), from that day on.
    


    

      
Numbering and file names
When the service numbers or names episodes differently from Sonarr.

      

        
        
        Used in the file name and the MKV title. Leave empty to use the name Sonarr gives the series.
      

      

        

          
          
          Sonarr's season = the service's, comma-separated.
        

        

          
          
          -1: service's E04 is Sonarr's E05.
        

      

      

        Season offset
        

          
          from season
          
        

        +3 from season 8: Sonarr's S08 is the service's S11, and so on for every season after, new ones too. "Seasons on the service" wins over it.
      

      

        
        
        Sonarr's episode = the service's, comma-separated. Wins over the settings above. Add .2 to take only part 2 of a split episode.
      

      

        Episodes split into parts
        
        Turn off when Sonarr counts each part as its own episode, and map the parts above instead.
        
        When every episode comes in that many parts: until they are all out, nothing is imported and it is tried again, so an episode is never imported half.
